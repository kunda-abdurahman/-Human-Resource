HR Attrition & Workforce Turnover
Understand where employee exits cluster and what questions HR should investigate next.
THE BUSINESS PROBLEM
Employee turnover creates recruitment and training costs, but a company-wide attrition figure can hide differences across departments, roles, tenure groups, or salary bands.
THE PROCESS
•	Calculate attrition using a clearly defined denominator and time period; distinguish employees who left from average headcount.
•	Compare rates by department, tenure, role, and salary band, while checking small-group sample sizes.
•	Estimate replacement cost only if the assumptions and cost components are documented.
•	Use findings to frame follow-up questions. SQL associations cannot establish that salary or a manager caused someone to leave.
HOW SUCCESS WOULD LOOK
HR can focus further investigation on a specific group and evaluate retention actions using consistent measures. Protect employee privacy and avoid exposing identifiable individual-level information in the portfolio.
WHERE TO GET DATA
Use anonymised, fictional, or public HR attrition data. Treat it as a practice dataset, not as evidence about a real employer.
